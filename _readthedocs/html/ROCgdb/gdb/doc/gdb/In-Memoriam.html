Appendix A In Memoriam ¶

The ROCGDB project mourns the loss of the following long-time contributors:

Fred Fish

Fred was a long-standing contributor to ROCGDB (1991-2006), and to Free Software in general. Outside of ROCGDB, he was known in the Amiga world for his series of Fish Disks, and the GeekGadget project.

Michael Snyder

Michael was one of the Global Maintainers of the ROCGDB project, with contributions recorded as early as 1996, until 2011. In addition to his day to day participation, he was a large driving force behind adding Reverse Debugging to ROCGDB.

Beyond their technical contributions to the project, they were also enjoyable members of the Free Software Community. We will miss them.
